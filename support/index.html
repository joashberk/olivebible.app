Support

Write to support@eido.net and we'll reply as soon as we can.

Where are my notes kept?

On your phone, inside the app. Nothing is sent anywhere. To keep a copy, open the vial › Settings › Export my notes and highlights.

How do I start over?

Open the vial › Settings › Erase everything on this phone. This can't be undone.

Which translations are included?

The World English Bible, the Berean Standard Bible, the King James Version and the American Standard Version. Hebrew and Greek word data comes from STEPBible (CC BY 4.0). See "About the text" in the app for full credits.

How does the oil work?

Your vial fills on its own as you study: finishing a topic, writing what you noticed, or spending time with a chapter. Nothing to collect, and no streaks.

Something looks wrong in a translation or a study

Please tell us the verse and what you noticed. Accuracy matters more to us than anything else.
