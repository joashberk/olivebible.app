Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

The short version

Olive Bible does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your notes, highlights, reading position, study progress, oil and settings are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. You can export your notes and highlights, or erase everything, from Settings in the app.

What the app downloads

The Bible text, study data and topic illustrations ship inside the app. When new topics are published, the app may download them from our public content release. These downloads do not include anything about you.

Sharing

When you share or copy a verse, the app hands plain text to your phone's own share sheet or clipboard. Where it goes next is up to you.

Children

Olive Bible collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if we add optional accounts for groups, this page will say exactly what is collected and why before it happens, and the app will ask first.
